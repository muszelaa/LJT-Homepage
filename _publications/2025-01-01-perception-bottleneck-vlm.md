**On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding**

Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

*arXiv*, 2025.

This study investigates the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) in the context of chart understanding, with associated code available in the Vision4Chart repository.
